— The atelier

Craft with intention, design with stillness.

Founded by architects and artisans, Nebula is not just an interior studio—it is a philosophy of restraint. We believe that true luxury lies in subtlety: the warmth of natural oak, the poetry of shadow, the precision of negative space.

Each project is a quiet collaboration, stripping the unnecessary to reveal the essential. Our work has been recognized across Milan, Paris, and Tokyo for its meditative minimalism.
