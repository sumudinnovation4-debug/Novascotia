Our Studio

Design that Breathes with You

At Nebula, we believe a space should be a living extension of its inhabitants — a choreography of light, texture, and proportion that tells a story unique to those who dwell within it.

Founded in 2012 by Isabelle Marchetti, our studio has grown into a collective of 28 designers, architects, and artisans who share an obsession with craft and a reverence for the rare. We work at the intersection of architecture and emotion.

Our Method

How We Bring Your Vision to Life

I
Phase One

Discovery & Vision

We begin with deep listening. Through extended conversations, site visits, and exploration of your lifestyle and aspirations, we map the invisible blueprint of your ideal space before a single line is drawn.

II
Phase Two

Concept Development

Our designers craft a conceptual world — mood boards, material palettes, spatial arrangements, and a narrative that ties every element into a coherent, emotionally resonant whole. We present, refine, and align.

III
Phase Three

Design & Detailing

Technical drawings, custom furniture specifications, lighting plans, and material schedules are developed in precise detail. We collaborate with architects, engineers, and artisans to ensure flawless execution.

IV
Phase Four

Procurement & Sourcing

We manage the sourcing, commissioning, and logistics of all elements — from bespoke pieces made in small workshops in Portugal to lighting crafted by French artisans. Nothing is left to chance.

V
Phase Five

Installation & Reveal

We oversee every moment of installation, dressing each space with care and precision. The reveal is not a handover — it's a ceremony. Your space, complete and breathing, ready to begin its story.
