About Studio Nova

Founded in 2025, we are a collective of designers, architects, and thinkers.

Design as a narrative

We believe spaces shape behaviour, mood, and connection. Studio Nova was born from a desire to move beyond trends and create interiors with emotional resonance.

Our approach balances timeless proportions with contemporary sensitivity. Every project begins with listening — to the architecture, to the light, to you.
