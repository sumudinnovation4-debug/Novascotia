Our Story

Design with
Soul

Founded in 2012 by Isabelle Laurent, Maison Nova is a New York-based interior design studio with a reputation for creating spaces of enduring elegance and quiet luxury.

(our philosophy)

Beauty that
lives and breathes

We believe that great interior design is not about following trends — it's about creating spaces that feel genuinely alive. Our process begins with deep listening: understanding how our clients move through their days, what brings them calm, what inspires them to gather and celebrate.

Every material we choose, every piece we source, every detail we specify carries intention. The result is spaces that are unmistakably yours — and unmistakably timeless.

Our Values

◎

Timelessness Over Trend

We design for decades, not seasons. Every choice is made with longevity in mind — spaces that grow richer as they're lived in.

◈

Radical Listening

Before we put pen to paper, we listen. Your story, your rhythms, your desires become the foundation of every design decision.

◇

Material Integrity

We work exclusively with materials of genuine quality — natural stone, aged brass, hand-woven textiles, solid timber. No substitutes.

○

Conscious Luxury

Opulence and responsibility are not opposites. We source sustainably, partner with ethical makers, and design for longevity.

(milestones)

Our Journey

2012

Studio Founded

Isabelle Laurent opens the studio in a Tribeca loft with a team of three, taking on the first residential commission in Manhattan.

2015

First Hospitality Project

Completion of the acclaimed Arden Boutique Hotel, earning recognition in Architectural Digest and Wallpaper* magazine.

2018

London Office Opens

International expansion begins with a West London studio and our first European residential commissions.

2021

IDSA Award for Excellence

Recognised as Interior Design Studio of the Year by the International Design Society of America.

2024

Paris Atelier

Our third international presence opens in the Marais, anchoring our European design operations.
